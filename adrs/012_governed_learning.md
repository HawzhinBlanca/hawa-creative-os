# ADR-012: Learn through governed rules, examples, templates, and evaluations

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Context

Creative feedback is contextual and sometimes contradictory. Continuous self-modification or immediate fine-tuning can turn one preference into a damaging global behavior.

## Decision

Record structured feedback, use task-scoped corrections immediately, propose persistent rules with evidence, and require authorized activation. Approved work becomes positive memory; rejected work is negative-only. Changes pass regression evaluation.

## Consequences

Learning is transparent, reversible, and client-scoped. It improves more slowly than unconstrained automation but avoids silent drift.

## Alternatives considered

Continuous fine-tuning and autonomous prompt editing: rejected. Never learning: wastes feedback.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit only when executable evidence shows that the decision no longer meets reliability, editability, security, or office-operation goals.
