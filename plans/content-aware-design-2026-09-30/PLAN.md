### Claude additions triage

- Gaze and sky/horizon proposals: reuse existing landmark/local evidence where available;
  treat as uncertain candidates, not inferred semantic certainty. Never flip photos.
- Optical edges/grid/radii and resolution: include in bounded W5 refinement and failure
  qualification. A single global grid/margin or 1.5x cap is not a universal taste metric.
- Repetition against last three approved designs: W6 scoped preference feature, subordinate
  to content and explicit style. Genuine labels and held-out canary still required.
- Perceptual tints: exact-palette validator currently forbids undeclared tones. Add an
  approved bounded tint policy and contrast/native evidence before using them. Numeric
  delta-E suggestions are experiments, not universal safe bounds.
- Opaque scrims can hide the lower field, and full-bleed heroes limit exposed background:
  evaluate visible rendered surfaces jointly with composition, not just a color token.

Earlier research baseline findings and ADR180's initial hero-only wording describe
dated inspections/decisions; this explicit owner clarification supersedes that limit.
