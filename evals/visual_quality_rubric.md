# Blind Visual Quality Rubric

Reviewers do not see provider/model identity. Score each dimension 1–5 and mark any critical failure.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brief fulfillment | misses core request | fulfills most requirements | complete, precise, no invented content |
| Brand fit | clearly off-brand | acceptable/generic | unmistakably client-specific without imitation artifacts |
| Composition/hierarchy | confusing | readable | controlled, purposeful, high-end hierarchy |
| Originality | cliché/near-template | competent | fresh idea appropriate to task |
| Typography | broken/weak | readable | polished rhythm, scale, spacing, RTL/LTR behavior |
| Imagery/material | artifacts/irrelevant | usable | coherent lighting/crop/material and strong role in concept |
| Cultural/language fit | inappropriate | neutral | native, context-aware, respectful |
| Editability | effectively flat | major elements editable | every likely revision is local and semantic |
| Multi-format resilience | breaks | needs moderate adjustment | adapts without losing idea/hierarchy |
| Repairability | full regeneration needed | some local repair | clear node/asset-level repairs |

## Critical failures

- wrong client/project;
- invented or altered protected fact/copy;
- wrong/deformed official logo;
- unreadable or semantically reordered Sorani/Arabic;
- missing editable source;
- prohibited content/asset;
- major task requirement omitted;
- leaked material from another client.

## Pairwise choice

After scoring, choose A, B, tie, or both unacceptable. Explain the decisive visual/operational reason in one or two sentences. Preference without reason is not accepted as evaluation evidence.
