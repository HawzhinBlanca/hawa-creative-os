Institutional Quality Framework

Standards of Higher Education
& Institutional Integrity

01
Standard I

Mission & Strategic Objectives

The institutional mission must guide curriculum, resource allocation, and student learning goals. The institution demonstrates periodic evaluation of strategic KPIs and systematic stakeholder review.

Required Evidence: Strategic Plan, Published KPIs & Minutes of Review
02
Standard II

Governance & Accountability

Clear organizational structures and job descriptions. An independent governing body oversees policies and multi-year budgets, strictly prohibiting conflicts of interest and ensuring leadership accountability.

Required Evidence: Institutional Bylaws, Annual Audits & Signed Ethics Pledges
03
Standard III

Academic Leadership & Programs

A Chief Academic Officer (CAO/Provost) directly oversees academic program integrity, credential verification, transparent admissions criteria, and continuous assessment of student learning outcomes.

Required Evidence: Faculty Credentials, Program Audits & Assessment Reports
